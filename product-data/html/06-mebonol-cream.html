مراحل التئام الجروح ودور ميبونول — Healing Phases

1. Cleaning phase — Inhibits microbial growth (Flavenols & bioflavenoid)

  • Effective as silver sulfadiazin in reduce of bacterial count.
  • Bactericidal against pseudomonas aeruginosa, kelbsiella…
  • Antimicrobial activity against common skin pathogens (Streptococcus spp & Staphylococcus spp).
  • Antifungal: candida albicans.
  • Effect as or more than combination of Erythromycin & Amoxicillin.

2. Inflammatory phase — Anti-inflammatory (Caffeic acid phenethyl ester, prostanoids, bradykinase)

  • Blocking of thromboxane and bradykinin.
  • Reducing neutrophil infiltration.
  • Reduce odema.
  • Reduce of lipoxygenase pathway of arachidonic acid.
  • Inhibit of non enzymatic mediator — reduce isoprosten.
  • Inhibit of Prostaglandin E2.
  • Anti-histamine: Mag. block of histamine release.
  • Anti-histamine: Flavonoids block the release of histamine by means of cell membrane lipid stabilization.

3. Proliferation phase — healing (collagen, amino acid, mucopolysaccharides)

  • Stimulation physiological regeneration and epithelisation.
  • Increase the rate of neovascularization.
  • Increase deposition of hyaluronan (major component of extracellular matrix).
  • Increase migration of newly formed capillaries.
  • Increase Stimulation fibroblast and connective tissue formation.

4. Remodeling phase — (Panthenol)

  • Metabolized in the skin to pantothenic acid which is a Co-enzyme needed to every releasing reaction in the human body.
