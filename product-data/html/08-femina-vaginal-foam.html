الخلفية العلمية (من العرض التقديمي)

Vaginal Immune System

  • Vaginal Lactobacilli
  • Vaginal PH
  • Vaginal discharge

Vaginal Lactobacilli

  • Represents 95% of healthy vaginal bacteria.
  • There are several types of lactobacilli but the most important type is which produces Hydrogen Peroxide.
  • Hydrogen peroxide inhibits growth of harmful bacteria (e.g: E.coli) and HIV in vitro.
  • Estrogen improves lactobacilli colonization by enhancing epithelial cells producing glycogen.

Vaginal PH

  • Normal vaginal PH is 3.8-4.5
  • Lactic acid produced by lactobacilli is the main cause of the acidic PH, so interruption of lactobacilli will upset the vaginal PH balance and vice versa.
  • Acidic vaginal PH helps limit the growth of other organisms and inhibits adherence of bacteria to vaginal epithelial cells.

Vaginal Discharge

  • A normal vagina is constantly kept moist by its slick, slippery and savory natural discharge.
  • Normal physiological discharges helps to clean the vagina as well as keep it lubricated and free from infection and other germs.
  • Healthy vaginal discharge smells mild earthy, it certainly doesn't smell strong foul odor.

Factors that corrupt normal vaginal immune system

  • Medicine: Antibiotics wipe out good bacteria and increase the risk of infection; contraceptives or any hormone containing drugs.
  • Menstrual cycle: due to hormonal fluctuations which alter PH-balance.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: the hormone levels alter PH too.
  • After sexual intercourse: semen is of alkaline PH and may cause irritation.
  • Emotional stress.
  • Poor diet and insufficient sleep hours.
  • Highly perfumed or alkaline vaginal cleansers.

Vaginitis Types

  • Infectious — Non STD vaginitis: Bacterial vaginosis, Candidiasis.
  • Infectious — STD vaginitis: Trichomonas vaginalis, Chlamydia trachomatis, Neisseria Gonorrhea.
  • Non infectious: Atrophic vaginitis, Allergic vaginitis, Vaginitis due to abortion or delivery.

Vaginitis Symptoms

  • An increase in the amount of discharge.
  • A change in the color or smell of discharge.
  • Irritation.
  • Itchiness.
  • Burning around the vagina.
  • Discomfort during urination or sexual intercourse may also occur.
