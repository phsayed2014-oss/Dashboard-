الدراسات والبيانات العلمية لـ Octopirox

Antidandruff Action in Hair Tonics

  • Hair tonic with 0.1% octopirox vs Placebo (40% isopropanol / 60% water) — decrease in incidence of dandruff (%) over 0-42 days treatment time.

Antidandruff action in shampoo form

  • Shampoo with 0.75% octopirox vs Placebo shampoo — % decrease in incidence of dandruff over 0-42 treatment days (application once weekly).

Adsorption to Hair keratin

  • The increase of concentration of octopirox is accompanied by increase in the amount adsorbed to hair keratin.
  • 3 min treatment of human hair (octopirox + 15% alkylether sulphate sod. sulph) — adsorbed amount (ug/g hair) vs Octopirox % in shampoo (0.5 – 1 – 1.5).
  • pH has only a slight effect on adsorption of octopirox to hair (pH 5-8).

Antibacterial Spectrum (Trial done at Difco lab. Detroit. USA)

  • Gram-positive: Staph. aureus, Micrococcus luteus (1 strain), Streptoc. pyogenes (2), Bac. subtilis (1).
  • Gram-negative: Escherichia coli (9), Salmonella var. species (8), Proteus var. species (9), Klebsiella var. species (3), Enterobacter var. species (4), Shigella flexneri (1), Pseudomonas aeruginosa (8), Haemophilus influenzae (1), Corynebact. var. species (2), Past. multocida (1).
  • MIC range tested: 4 – 1000 µg/ml.

Antifungal Spectrum — MIC for most important fungal species bet. (0.5-4 mcg/ml)

  • Trichophyton rubrum (5 strains), Trichophyton mentagrophytes (4), Other Trichophyton species (1), Microsporum canis (1), Other Microsporum species (2), Epidermophyton floccosum (1), Candida albicans (5), Candida tropicalis (3), Other yeastlike fungi (3), Aspergillus niger (1), Aspergillus fumigatus (2), Other moulds (4).

In vitro activity against Pityrosporum ovale (orbiculare)

  • Efficacy against Malassezia (pityrosporum ovale), the main cause of dandruff — fungus growth score vs octopirox concentration 1–500 µg/ml compared with control.

Is pyrithione zinc better than ketoconazole?

  • During the second cross-over study week, the 1% pyrithione zinc conditioning shampoo was significantly better than the 2% ketoconazole shampoo at improving hair frizz/flyaway (p=0.0053) and hair smoothness (p=0.0041).

الخلفية العلمية: القشرة — Dandruff

What is Dandruff?

  • Dandruff is a chronic, non-inflammatory condition characterized by excessive scaling of scalp.
  • The scales is the dry sebum secreted by sebaceous gland of the skin.
  • Normally present in the scalp. Becomes abnormal when excessive.

What Are Dandruff Treatments?

  • Dandruff treatments are ways you can get rid of or control itching and flaking on your scalp. The first two to try are brushing your hair and using a dandruff shampoo.

Incidence

  • Dandruff is more common at puberty and young adults.
  • More than 50% of population have a moderate to severe dandruff.

The scaling process

  • The epidermal cells on the scalp slough off continually just as on other parts of the body.
  • However the rate of epidermal cell turnover in normal individuals is greater on the scalp than on other parts of the body.
  • Dandruff flakes often appear around a hair shaft due to the epithelial growth at the base of the hair. The hair restricts the elimination of sloughed keratin, creating a visible scale.

What makes dandruff excessive?

  • Once it can be noticed it is considered abnormal.
  • Sebum secretion of hair covered scalp → (Microbes Lipases) → Free Fatty Acids → (Microbes peroxidases, light/air) → Lipoperoxides → Chronic irritant effect → Acceleration of mitosis in the germ layer → Parakeratosis → Dandruff.

Chronic irritation caused by — A: Internal factors

  • Microbial infections: Fungal or yeast (mainly Malassezia).
  • Bacterial infection which: Decrease fungal eradication; Increase resistance to antidandruff treatment; Increase relapse (recurrence).
  • N.B: many strains of microorganism normally present in the skin; its abnormal multiplication & growth only occur under certain circumstance.

B: External factors (environmental)

  • Air pollution.
  • Calcareous water.
  • Extreme temperature changes.
  • Dyes.
  • Vigorous brushing and using hot hairdryers.
  • Excessive use of hair sprays and other cosmetics.
  • Reference: 1. Ponadea and Lupake (1979). 2. M-Loden et al. (2000).

5 reasons why you have dandruff

  • 1. Sensitive Scalp: Extremely oily skin or skin that reacts strongly to certain hair care products may develop dandruff.
  • 2. Dry Winters: The cold outdoor and warm indoors cause an imbalance that dries out the scalp and makes it itchy/flaky.
  • 3. Scalp Fungus: Yeast-like fungus such as Malassezia Globosa feed on skin oils and gives rise to dandruff.
  • 4. Skin Disorders: Dry skin diseases like Psoriasis, Seborrheic Dermatitis or Eczema often cause dandruff to develop.
  • 5. Stress: Glucocorticoids generated due to stress, change the makeup of scalp cells and cause dandruff.

Symptoms

  • Excessive Scaling.
  • Erythema.
  • Itching.

Malassezia — trigger & response

  • Trigger events: Malassezia Yeast + Immune System → Dandruff / Psoriasis.
  • Inflammatory response: Skin Inflammation (inflamed cells, peri-follicle).
  • Symptoms: Skin hyper-proliferation → Flakes; Hair peri-follicular atrophy → Constriction, Roughness; Promote telogen phase (?) → Hair Loss.

علاج القشرة — Treatment of dandruff

General

  • There is no cure for dandruff, only control of the condition.
  • Other approaches are available which decrease visible dandruff.

Methods used to reduce the visible flaking of dandruff

  • 1- Keratolytic agent to dissolve dandruff flakes, as salicylic acid and sulfur to dissolve keratin and facilitate its removal from the scalp in smaller particles.
  • 2- Cleaning the hair and scalp frequently: Shampooing the hair and scalp at short intervals, perhaps daily, should control most forms of dandruff.
  • 3- Cytostatic agents e.g. selenium sulfide and zinc pyrithione, that reduce the rate of epidermal turnover.
  • 4- Dispersing the scales into smaller subunits so that they are less visible, several agents such as coal tar.
  • 5- Reduction of microbial count by antifungals as ketoconazole and pyridone derivatives (ciclopirox, octopirox, rilopirox).

Notes — Keratolytics

  • Resorcinol acts as a keratolytic by its irritant effect which causes vesicle formation in the stratum corneum.
  • Sulfur is believed to function by an inflammatory process causing increased sloughing of cells.
  • Salicylic acid may be caused by lowering the pH of the skin causing increased hydration of keratin thus facilitating its loosening and removal.
  • Vehicles composition, time of contact and concentration are important considerations to the success of a keratolytic.

الخلفية العلمية: التهاب الجلد الدهني — Seborrheic Dermatitis

Definition

  • An inflammatory scaling disease of the scalp, face, and occasionally other areas. Despite the name, the composition and flow of sebum are usually normal.

Symptoms and Signs

  • In severe disease, yellow-red, scaling papules appear along the hairline, behind the ears, in the external auditory canals, on the eyebrows, on the bridge of the nose, in the nasolabial folds, and over the sternum.
  • Marginal blepharitis with dry yellow crusts and conjunctival irritation may be present.
  • Seborrheic dermatitis does not cause hair loss.
  • Genetic and climatic factors seem to affect the incidence and severity of the disease; it is usually worse in winter.
  • The prognosis is better than in atopic dermatitis.

Treatment

  • In adults, zinc pyrithione, selenium sulfide, sulfur and salicylic acid, tar shampoo should be used daily or every other day until dandruff is controlled and twice/week thereafter.
  • A corticosteroid lotion (eg, 0.01% fluocinolone acetonide solution or 0.025% triamcinolone lotion) should be rubbed into the scalp or other hairy areas twice daily until scaling and redness are controlled.
  • Hydrocortisone 1% cream rubbed in bid or tid will rapidly improve seborrheic dermatitis of the postauricular areas, nasolabial folds, eyelid margins and bridge of the nose; the cream is then used once daily if needed.
  • Studies have suggested that topical antifungals (anti-yeast compounds) may be effective in some patients with seborrheic dermatitis and have focused attention on the possible role of pityrosporum ovale (a common lipophilic yeast normally present in follicles) as a cause for this disease.

Pityriasis versicolor — distribution

  • Fair skin / Dark skin presentations — distribution map: Most common (chest/upper back), Common, Uncommon, Rare.
